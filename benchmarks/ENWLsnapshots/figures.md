# ENWL Snapshots — volt-var / volt-watt visualisations (PU)

Smart-inverter (AS/NZS 4777.2) behaviour across one day of varying solar, 
per feeder. Generated by `plot_snapshots.jl` from the `*_result_pu.json` results.

> Note: with volt-watt `p_ref = S_MAX`, available solar (`p_max`) is not 
> enforced as the active-power upper bound, so Σ P stays near rated even at 
> night — visible as the gap to Σ P available in the daily plot.
