Device topology and parameter indexing

A vector field such as p_nom, i_max or v_min has one entry per something, and that something depends on the device and its wiring: a phase, a coil between two terminals, a conductor, or a pair of phases. This page states the rule for every indexed field. integrity_check and spec_conformance_check enforce these rules; they report mismatches and never reorder or repair a case.

What these checks do not establish

A correctly sized array says nothing about grounding adequacy or electrical equivalence. Those are separate analyses.

Three terms

  • Phase order is the terminal_map order with the identified neutral removed. Bus phase order is terminal_names order with the neutral removed. The neutral does not have to be the last entry.
  • Cyclic coils connect map position k to position k+1, wrapping at the end. A DELTA on ["a","b","c"] has coils ab, bc, ca.
  • A two-terminal SINGLE_PHASE load, generator or IBR has one coil, in either orientation, whether it is phase-to-neutral or phase-to-phase.

For example, a WYE load on terminal_map = ["a","b","c","n"] has three phase-to-neutral coils, so p_nom has three entries, for a, b and c. With terminal_map = ["n","c","a"] it has two, for c and a.

Empty, absent and scalar values

  • Absent optional bounds are allowed.
  • An empty P/Q, current or apparent-power limit array on a generator, IBR or source means "no authored limits". A nonempty partial vector is an error.
  • cost and filter arrays must cover every indexed quantity.
  • No scalar is broadcast for capabilities, costs, source references or bus bounds. A scalar bus v_min/v_max is rejected at ingest.
  • The scalar b_filter_shunt is not a phase array. Filter arrays are checked for shape only; the check does not assert that the OPF stamps a physical filter.
  • vpn_* on a bus without an identified neutral is reported as unassessed.

Load-model coefficient shapes are checked by domain_rules_check; all other shapes by integrity_check. The JSON schema may impose additional representation requirements.

How roles are resolved

A declared terminal_conventions block takes precedence over naming guesses and over a stale in-memory neutral_terminal. Equivalent labels such as n, 4 and return give the same indexing result once declared. Without a declaration, roles are inferred and W.CONV.TERMINAL_ROLES_INFERRED is raised.

  • Phase permutations and reversed single-phase pairs are valid. I.TMAP.PERMUTED_ORDER may describe the ordering; it is informational.
  • An explicit earth conductor is outside these device models and raises E.TMAP.DEVICE_ROLES. It is never counted as a phase.
  • An implicit-reference WYE (no neutral in its map) raises W.INT.WYE_WITHOUT_NEUTRAL. No implicit return is inferred.
  • Duplicate terminals in a map and a neutral in a DELTA map have their own diagnostics.
  • Load-model coefficient families that the chosen model does not read are reported for information.
  • OpenDSS intake uses n_ref for a separate, perfectly grounded node-0 return. A bus may carry both the conductor neutral n and n_ref; each device map selects its own return, and n remains the bus neutral for voltage bounds. See the intake semantics. Other multiple-neutral declarations are reported as unassessed.

Findings

CodeMeaning
W.INT.DIM_MISMATCHAn indexed field has the wrong shape. The evidence lists the bus, topology, terminal map and positions, resolved roles and their provenance, the indexing domain, the allowed lengths, whether a scalar is allowed, and the observed shape
E.LOAD.*_ARITYThe same, for load-model fields
W.INT.DEVICE_INDEXING_UNASSESSEDRoles are overlapping, unclassified or ambiguous, so no expected dimension is computed (status = "indeterminate"). The declaration problem itself is reported by the E.CONV.*/W.CONV.* checks, and unknown references by E.INT.*
W.SPEC.CONFIG_ARITY, W.SPEC.INV_TMAP_ARITYThe terminal-map length differs from the data-model configuration arity. A reduced-phase device can have correctly sized arrays and still receive this conformance warning

These are static validation passes over raw data. They do not guarantee that every analysis or solver entry point accepts schema-invalid input.
