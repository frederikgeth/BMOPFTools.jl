Analysis result keys

Reference for the structured results in analyze(net).results. For what the passes are and how to read the report, start with Analysis & reports; for the meaning of a finding code, use the finding-code reference. This page documents selected keys, not every key a pass emits; keys not documented here may change in any release. See Versioning for the package's compatibility policy.

Keys are written results[:pass]["key"], where results = analyze(net).results; a nested key continues the chain, as in results[:connectivity]["structure"]["whole_network"]. Each section opens with a table of the pass's top-level keys. "Conditional" marks a key that is present only when the stated condition holds.

capacitorDicttotal and q_rated_total

Voltage levels

results[:voltage_levels] propagates nominal voltages from the sources through transformer ratios and clusters buses into levels.

KeyTypeMeaning
bus_voltage_mapDictBus id → per-conductor (phase-to-reference) nominal voltage in V
levelsDictLevel label (e.g. LV_231V) → nominal_v, nominal_kv, and the buses, lines, loads and generators at that level with their counts
n_levelsIntNumber of levels
voltage_basis, voltage_basis_noteStringAlways phase_to_reference; the note states that balanced line-to-line values are √3 times these
unassigned_busesVectorBuses no voltage source reaches
neutral_only_busesVectorBuses that carry only a neutral or earth conductor
transformer_transitionsVectorOne record per transformer (per winding pair for n_winding): id, subtype, vector_group, phase_relation, bus_from, bus_to, v_from, v_to, level_from, level_to
high_leg_banksVectorOne record per power unit paired with a center-tapped lighting unit (below)
high_leg_unitsVectorPower-unit ids from high_leg_banks of every status except undetermined
mixed_phase_to_neutral_busesVectorSecondary buses of verified high-leg banks

The map holds one per-conductor value per bus. Three cases need care:

  • A phase-to-phase single-phase winding carries a line-to-line v_nom; the map holds the corresponding per-conductor value.
  • An n_winding DELTA winding's v_nom is its line-to-line coil voltage; the map again holds the per-conductor value of the bus it lands on.
  • On a high-leg (four-wire) delta bus no single value describes every conductor. The map holds the center-tapped leg voltage and the bus is listed in mixed_phase_to_neutral_buses.

High-leg delta banks

Each entry of results[:voltage_levels]["high_leg_banks"]:

FieldMeaning
power_unitId of the single_phase unit that creates the high leg
lighting_unitId of the center_tap unit whose legs it joins
bus_from, bus_toPrimary and secondary bus of the power unit
high_legSecondary conductor of the power unit that is not on a lighting leg
statusVerdict, below
secondary_amplitude_ratioPower-unit secondary voltage over the bank's line-to-line voltage (twice the leg voltage); 0 when the power winding has no voltage across it; nothing when the phasors are not established or the bank is undetermined
secondary_amplitude_range[low, high] of that ratio over all tap positions when a unit has a free tap (tap_min < tap_max); nothing otherwise
statusMeaning
verifiedThe primaries close the delta and the power unit's secondary is the bank's line-to-line amplitude within 10 % at every tap position. The high leg sits at √3 times the leg voltage to neutral.
amplitude_mismatchThe primaries' directions close the delta, but the amplitude is off by more than 10 % at every tap position (a wrong rating, or a weak source phase), or the power winding's two primary terminals trace to one source conductor (ratio 0). Not a high leg; reported as W.VOLT.XFMR_RATIO.
unverifiedThe primary phasors are not established (no source declares the angle of a winding's conductors, as behind another transformer), or free tap positions disagree. Not a high leg; the unit is kept out of level propagation; no Finding.
undeterminedThe power unit's primary is tied to the lighting unit's on some but not all conductors. Not a high leg; kept out of level propagation; the pair is listed in results[:connectivity]["undetermined_transformer_banks"].

Entries of results[:connectivity]["undetermined_transformer_banks"] carry bus_to, units, primary_buses and a reason:

reasonMeaning
partial_primary_tieA tie carries some but not all of the conductors a winding's primary uses; the units stay separate branches
no_common_supply_pointEach unit shares a supply bus with the next, but no single bus holds them all

Two units are one bank when their supply points share a bus: a winding's supply point is the set of buses, reached by following conductors terminal by terminal through closed switches, at which every primary terminal, the neutral included, is present (supply_buses in transformer_bank_groups). A full primary disconnect between the units does not split the bank; a tie on a conductor neither winding uses, or a neutral bond alone, is no evidence. Verification traces each primary terminal's conductor through the terminal maps of lines and closed switches to the magnitude and angle a voltage source declares for it, then takes each secondary voltage as that phasor times the unit's effective turns ratio, nameplate ratio and tap, as the solver builds it. A conductor no source declares is at zero only when one of its terminals is a perfectly grounded return terminal or sits on a source bus; a shunt to earth is a path, not a voltage. Phase labels are never evidence of an angle, and nothing depends on bus names or switch direction. In-phase primaries, primaries on the same phase, a line-to-line beside a line-to-neutral primary, or one reversed winding do not form a bank and are reported as ordinary level conflicts. A power unit's own ratio is still checked against twice the leg level.

Transformer phase evidence

Transformer phase reporting separates authored winding connections and nameplate declarations from the derived ideal effective relation. Inventory and voltage transitions retain string labels and add structured status, phase correspondence, orientation and coil evidence. A ? suffix never means clock zero. Use transformer_phase_relation for a directed winding pair or pass phase_order to the standalone inventory/voltage passes for custom labels.

Topology structure

results[:connectivity]["structure"] groups graph counts. Every graph count includes declared buses, including isolated buses. Closed switches, lines, and transformer winding branches are physical edges; open switches are absent. Invalid or self-loop branches are excluded and counted in n_skipped_branches. A zone is a connected component formed by lines, closed switches, and galvanically continuous transformers; isolating transformers connect zones.

KeyContents
whole_networkBus/component/edge counts; physical and simple cycle ranks, parallel excess, transformer- and DC-mediated cycle ranks, and branch_scope
voltage_tiersOne count record per source-propagated voltage label, including unassigned where needed
galvanic_zonesOne record per zone with its minimum-bus anchor, voltage labels, cycle counts, and number of incident isolating transformers
parallel_linesParallel-line evidence (Redundancy), restricted to declared buses
cycle_closing_branchesUp to ten deterministic spanning-forest closing branches, with member IDs and endpoints; these are graph witnesses, not defect assignments
conductor_pathsTerminal-level components through mapped lines and closed switches, with voltage-tier counts and bounded load-terminal witnesses. Transformer winding terminals and voltage-source terminals act as boundary ports; transformer conversion is not inferred
n_cross_tier_edges, n_skipped_branchesEdges omitted from within-tier graphs, and invalid/self-loop branches omitted from graph counts

For each graph, cycle_rank = n_physical_edges - n_buses + n_components and cycle_rank = simple_cycle_rank + parallel_excess. The simple graph retains one edge per bus pair. transformer_mediated_cycle_rank is the whole-network cycle rank without DC links, less the sum of within-zone cycle ranks; dc_mediated_cycle_rank counts the cycles that DC links add. A tier contains only edges whose endpoints have the same source-propagated voltage-level label; n_cross_tier_edges counts the excluded branches. Unassigned buses form an explicit unassigned tier.

whole_network["branch_scope"] states what one physical branch is in the cycle counts and witnesses; the mesh verdict uses the same branches, so n_extra_edges + neutral_only_cycle_rank equals whole_network["cycle_rank"]. Cycles and parallel lines are descriptive evidence, not automatic data-quality Findings.

Switch-state bus graph scenarios

results[:connectivity]["switch_scenarios"] compares the supplied switch-state snapshot with a fixed backbone (all switch edges removed) and an all-closed envelope (all valid switch edges included). Every view retains all declared buses, including isolated buses, and counts each physical parallel branch separately. The three views report bus, component, physical and simple edge, physical and simple cycle-rank, parallel-excess, source-containing component, and bus/load-without-source-path counts. The all-closed envelope is a graph bound; it may combine mutually exclusive switch positions.

transition_counts["classifications"] counts all individually assessed switches: component_join for an open switch between declared components, cycle_closure or parallel_closure for an open switch within one component, bridge for a closed switch whose opening splits a component, and alternate_path for a closed switch whose opening leaves the component connected. Each transition's component and physical cycle-rank deltas are reported in witnesses, with at most five witnesses per class, sorted by switch ID. Counts cover every assessed switch. For ties to a component without a source path and bridges that remove such a path, witnesses include affected bus and load counts. The result also counts ties between source-containing components.

The result is inapplicable when there are no switches. It is indeterminate if any switch lacks a Boolean open_switch, has a missing endpoint, references an undeclared bus, or is a self-loop; the invalid IDs appear in switch_scenarios["assessment"]["invalid_switch_ids"]. Counterfactual counts are omitted in that case. Incomplete terminal maps do not prevent a bus-graph assessment. switch_scenarios["assessment"]["skipped_branch_ids"] lists invalid or self-loop declared branches excluded from graph counts; switch_scenarios["switch_counts"]["n_assessed"] is zero when switch state comparison is indeterminate.

switch_scenarios["conductor"] separately compares mapped terminal paths for the declared, fixed-backbone, and all-closed views. Its counts include mapped conductor edges, path components, components containing a declared voltage-source or transformer winding port, and bus/load terminals without a path to such a boundary. Each switch transition reports its change in path components and the number of load terminals gaining or losing a boundary path. This can expose a missing phase or neutral path even when the bus graph stays connected. The path_merge, path_split, and no_path_change class counts cover every assessed switch; JSON carries up to five sorted witnesses per class. A multi-conductor switch changes all mapped terminal pairs together. cross_layer_counts counts combinations such as cycle_closure|path_merge, which show when the bus and mapped-conductor graphs respond differently to the same switch. The implementation uses bridge precomputation when pairs occupy different path components and an exact group-removal calculation otherwise; switch_scenarios["conductor"]["assessment"]["n_group_cut_fallbacks"] reports how many switches needed that calculation.

The conductor layer is inapplicable if bus terminal names or any required line/switch, load, or boundary-port map is incomplete; its assessment lists the affected record IDs. It is indeterminate if no source or transformer boundary port exists. These statuses do not discard an applicable bus-graph result. Transformer ports mark path boundaries on each winding side; the analysis does not infer electrical continuity or phase conversion through the transformer.

Source-path counts use only declared voltage-source and load bus incidence. A graph path does not prove energization, switch operability, protection compatibility, phase or voltage compatibility, capacity, or synchronization. The W.CONN.MESHED and E.CONN.DISCONNECTED Findings describe only the declared snapshot; switch scenarios introduce no Findings.

Transformer banks and DC links

results[:connectivity]["transformer_banks"] lists single-phase units that join the same two buses on different conductors (three line-to-neutral units, an open-wye/open-delta pair, a center-tapped lighting unit with a power unit). Each entry gives bus_from, bus_to and the sorted units. A bank is one branch of the bus graph for radiality, and supplies the union of its legs' phases. A unit whose secondary phase conductors another unit already spans (the same pair, or the two legs of a center-tapped winding) is a parallel transformer: it is not a leg and closes a cycle.

results[:connectivity]["transformer_bank_groups"] gives the full resolution for every secondary bus with more than one single-phase unit: supply_buses (the shared supply point, defined above), bus_from (its first bus), bus_to, units (the legs), parallel_units, unassessed_units (maps that cannot be read), n_branches, and ambiguous, true when a unit partly overlaps the legs, as a–b beside a–n and b–n does. A center-tapped unit anchors its bank: a unit across one of its legs is the parallel one. The grouping is resolved for the whole pair before anything is counted and does not depend on component ids. Unit pairs whose grouping cannot be decided are listed in undetermined_transformer_banks (reasons).

results[:connectivity]["dc_links"] lists groups of AC buses whose converters share one DC island; the buses of a group are connected through it. In the degree and path metrics each DC island is a vertex of its own, joined to every AC bus it couples, so a hop over a DC island counts as two and the metrics do not depend on how the AC buses are named. longest_path_buses lists AC buses only; longest_path_vertices is the same walk with each DC island shown as dc_island:<label>. dc_link_islands gives the same groups with the dc_island label (its smallest dc_bus id), so two islands that link the same AC buses stay distinct. Cycles closed through a DC island are counted in results[:connectivity]["structure"]["whole_network"]["dc_mediated_cycle_rank"], not as transformer-mediated, and the closing-branch witness is the DC link, with an id of the form <dc_island>:<bus>|<bus>.

Zone topology

Each entry of results[:connectivity]["zones"] has label (the zone's source bus, or else its minimum bus id), topology, phases and n_buses. topology is three_phase, single_phase, split_phase, swer or other. A zone is swer only when a line or closed switch carries its one phase conductor alone and no branch in the zone carries a neutral: the earth return has to be witnessed by a branch. A two-wire section, or a branchless service bus behind a single-phase transformer, is single_phase. n_swer_zones and n_split_phase_zones count the tagged zones.

Geographic evidence

results[:connectivity]["spatial"] is present when buses carry coordinate fields. It describes coordinate quality, shared infrastructure, geometry/topology candidates and an evidence-bound cleanup queue; coincidence alone is not an error. Its keys and settings are in Spatial analysis settings & result keys; the reasoning is in Spatial diagnostics.

Diversity

results[:diversity] measures parameter spread per category. Uniform values across many components suggest synthetic or allocated data.

KeyTypeMeaning
loadDictP, Q and power-factor statistics, phase imbalance, configurations, models, and load repetition (below)
generatorDictGenerator spread
lineDictLength statistics
linecodeDictSeries-resistance statistics and the share with mutual coupling

Each category Dict carries analysed (whether it had data to assess) and, when assessed, symmetry_flag.

Load repetition counts identical ordered (p_nom, q_nom) declarations in results[:diversity]["load"]: n_total_loads, n_distinct_pq_tuples, n_excess_repetitions, n_repeated_groups, n_members_of_repeated_groups, largest_group_count, and up to five witnesses. Repetition does not establish duplicate customers.

Transformer utilization

Each transformer_utilisation entry has id, subtype, s_rating_va, s_load_va, utilisation_pct, estimate_status, and the fixed labels comparison_basis = "nominal_demand_to_nameplate" and modeled_limit_compliance = "unassessed". The downstream-load estimate excludes the transformer under analysis. If its from side remains reachable from its to side, estimate_status is upper_bound and the entry does not raise W.OPS.XFMR_OVERLOADED; parallel siblings and other alternate paths defeat the radial attribution. Otherwise estimate_status is radial_component. This is an aggregate nameplate screen; the per-coil estimate is the coil headroom in pre-flight.

Transformer winding caps

For two-winding subtypes the OPF turns s_rating into a per-coil cap. An n_winding transformer is different: only per-winding i_max (coil current) and s_max (coil apparent power, shared equally among phase legs) limit it. results[:operational]["winding_limit_coverage"] lists each winding's caps with field paths, and W.OPS.XFMR_UNCONSTRAINED flags windings with no finite supported cap. A cap can be absent or explicitly Inf; observations count both states, so an intentional unbounded declaration stays visible. Zero is a real restriction. Direct neutral-current caps are not assessed, and this is declared data, not a built or solved model. Other subtypes are reported as not applicable.

Feeder length and supply boundaries

results[:operational]["feeder_length"] reports, per galvanic zone, how far (by declared line length) the farthest bus is from the nearest supply boundary: a voltage source, or a transformer port with a source beyond it. For a chain of four 100 m lines fed from both ends this is 200 m (boundary_reach_m), while reach_m / anchor_extent_m measures 400 m from one end. I.OPS.FEEDER_LONG and I.OPS.FEEDER_SHORT compare it with per-band thresholds. Review genuine feeder size, unit conversions and the OpenDSS default length=1: copying 1 km as 1 m makes a line 1000 times too short; interpreting 1000 m as 1000 km upstream makes it 1000 times too long.

observations.impedance_basis_counts separates per-length length_scaled linecodes, inline absolute_section matrices and unresolved declarations. As in the model, inline matrices win when a line also names a linecode; inline_overrides_linecode counts those lines. Length errors scale linecode impedances, while inline matrices are absolute section totals and stay unchanged when length changes. The guide uses that context; for an all-inline zone it explains the distance error separately from electrical parameters. Per-km versus per-m impedance units deserve their own check even when declared lengths are correct.

Each transformer port records a port_role: source_beyond_port (a possible supply into the zone), no_source_beyond_port (load side) or unresolved. Two or more ports with a source beyond them mean parallel supply, which the report lists for review. Supported boundaries are voltage sources and untapped two-port n_winding, single_phase, wye_delta and delta_wye transformers whose voltage bases agree (source magnitudes within 5%, cycle ratios within 10⁻⁹). Anything else leaves the distance unassessed with a stated reason rather than a guess. The metric is graph distance; it does not compute voltage drop, flow or energization. Missing or invalid lengths leave it indeterminate; regularized electrical lengths suppress the threshold comparison.

Zones with several supply boundaries need their own threshold policy, since a short distance to one of many ports does not make the zone a service drop:

[operational.feeder_length.LV.multi_boundary]
reach_short_m = 0.0
reach_long_m = 1500.0

Impedance hint evidence

The impedance provenance hints (I.PROV.SEQ_DERIVED, I.PROV.DECOUPLED_PHASES, I.PROV.IMPEDANCE_TRANSFORM_*) keep their evidence in detail["interpretation"]; how to read each pattern is in Impedance provenance hints.

  • observations["by_linecode"][id] records n_conductors, phase_block_only (a conductor lies outside the inspected phase block) and additional_conductor_coupling_ratio (the relative coupling to it). The decoupled-phases hint adds phase_self_uniform, which separates uniform from unequal diagonal terms; the sequence hint adds the implied z1 and z0.
  • observations["pattern_comparison"] compares matrix statistics with the reference patterns.
  • The Kron hint's supporting_context counts the uses of each linecode: n_mv_hv_lines, n_lv_with_neutral_lines and n_unassessed_lines sum to n_other_or_unassessed_lines, each with bounded line-ID examples. Its implications carry the id known_mv_hv_context when every referenced use is a positively identified MV/HV line, unused_linecode_context when no line uses the linecode, and unassessed_use_context when some uses have incomplete or mixed voltage or map evidence.

Converter-generated source equivalents (linecodes whose source names a Thevenin or source impedance, e.g. from from_dss) are classified but never flagged; the report counts them. Compatibility details are in Provenance report compatibility.

Qualified nominal transformer coil headroom

results[:preflight]["transformer_coil_headroom"] exposes a nominal-demand estimate, separate from the supplied-result profile_solution(net, result).results[:transformer_loading] report. The same block is carried by execute_analysis, JSON analysis reports and the analyze_case recipe. Markdown includes the coil table and unassessed reasons; terminal reports show the stable preflight Findings.

The supported domain is one single_phase transformer bank in a connected radial network with exactly one upstream voltage source, explicit ordered branch maps, and phase-neutral SINGLE_PHASE or WYE loads. A case may use its documented n/N neutral naming convention or declared terminal roles; the report records which was used. Endpoint labels are never matched as a substitute for an absent map. Lines may be oriented either way and may explicitly permute phase labels. Fixed open switches are omitted from the supply graph; isolated buses make this whole-network screen inapplicable. Reverse-fed transformers, multiple banks or supplies, meshes (including parallel paths), phase-to-phase/delta loads, ZIP/exponential models, local generation, IBRs, separate shunts/capacitors, DC devices, control profiles and authored tap bounds/decisions are unassessed. The screen does not allocate the unsupported cases by phase count or bank totals.

For each mapped downstream coil, sum signed p_nom and q_nom and calculate S_nom = hypot(sum(P_nom), sum(Q_nom)). P must be nonnegative; signed Q permits nominal reactive cancellation. Constant-power, constant-current and constant-impedance loads are evaluated at their own nominal voltages; the latter two require finite positive v_nom. This assumes nominal voltage everywhere, without calculating voltage drops, neutral displacement or mutual coupling. Line/transformer losses, charging and magnetizing demand are omitted. Upstream loads are outside the transformer sum. Local sources and compensation are excluded rather than credited against demand. Source, voltage and current limits are unassessed. A fixed positive tap does not change this ideal nominal power accounting; tap bounds or control declarations receive no estimate.

Only this native single_phase phase-neutral bank uses s_rating / mapped_coil_count as the from-side nameplate cap. Missing, zero, negative or nonfinite ratings give an indeterminate result, never a zero cap. The estimator does not infer other subtypes' caps or touch solver constraints. Each estimated row records from/to terminals, source terminal, nominal P/Q/S, cap, utilization, headroom and individual load contributions with ordered source paths. The bank summary sums coil apparent magnitudes. Missing or ambiguous data yield no partial numeric allocation and a stable reason.

For example, a 100 kVA three-coil bank has a 33.3 kVA cap per coil. With 40 kW on coil 1 alone, nominal bank utilization is 40 %, but coil 1 is at 120 % with headroom −6.667 kVA. W.PRE.XFMR_COIL_HEADROOM flags negative nominal headroom; I.PRE.XFMR_COIL_UNASSESSED explains unsupported or incomplete cases. W.OPS.XFMR_OVERLOADED is the separate aggregate nominal-load warning above 90 %.

Positive headroom does not prove feasibility and negative headroom does not prove infeasibility. The result records both limitations, its assumptions and solver_run = false. It neither retries a solve nor edits the network. This is a nominal-demand screen. Assessing a solved network also requires checking its equations, operating limits and recovered quantities.
