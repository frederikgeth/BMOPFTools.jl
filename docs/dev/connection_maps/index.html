Branch and winding connection maps

Lines, switches and transformers attach to buses through terminal maps. This page states which maps are valid for each element. integrity_check verifies the references and shapes; spec_conformance_check verifies the conductor roles. Neither pass repairs, reorders or truncates a map.

What these checks do not establish

A valid map does not show that grounding is adequate or that two models are electrically equivalent.

The two questions

  1. Does each endpoint exist and have a usable shape? Unknown buses and terminals raise E.INT.UNKNOWN_BUS and E.INT.UNKNOWN_TERMINAL. Entries that are not objects, missing or non-string bus identifiers, and empty or malformed maps raise E.INT.CONNECTION_SHAPE. Duplicate terminals within one map have their own diagnostic. The evidence names the endpoint or the winding index.
  2. Do the conductor roles at each position make sense for this element? The answer depends on the element, as the table shows.

Rules by element

ElementEndpointsRule
Linebus_from/to, terminal_map_from/toBoth maps and the impedance matrix agree in size. Position k connects conductors of the same role
Switch, open or closedsameEqual map lengths; the same role at each position
single_phasebothMatching positive coil counts. With a neutral in the map, coils are phase-to-neutral; a neutral-free two-terminal map is one phase pair; other phase-only maps are implicit-reference coils
center_tapbothHV is [phase, neutral] or a phase pair. LV is [phase, neutral, phase] in leg-1, midpoint, leg-2 order
wye_delta, delta_wyebothThe wye side's phase order excludes its neutral, wherever it sits. The delta side contains only phases. Wye phase count equals delta coil count, at least two
single_phase_autotransformerbothExactly one regulating coil per side, phase-to-neutral or phase-to-phase. Explicit shared reference bushings have matching roles
open_delta_regulatorbothThree phases per side, with an optional identified neutral
n_windingevery windings[k]At least two windings; WYE or DELTA configuration; equal positive phase counts; a DELTA winding has at least two phases and no neutral

Lines and switches: same role, any label

Phase labels need not match across a line or switch. Phase subsets, arbitrary phase permutations and a single neutral-to-neutral metallic link are all valid. I.TMAP.CROSS_PHASE_LINE and I.TMAP.PERMUTED_ORDER report them for information.

What is not valid is a phase at one end meeting a neutral at the other at the same position. That raises E.TMAP.PHASE_TO_NEUTRAL, with both endpoint records and the offending position. A map that repeats the neutral at every position raises it too, alongside the duplicate-terminal diagnostic.

Transformers: each winding on its own terms

A transformer may change phase labels, winding connection, terminal count and neutral accessibility. E.TMAP.WINDING_ROLES therefore reports a violation of the subtype's own rule in the table. It never requires equal conductor roles across independent windings, and there is no through-neutral requirement.

An autotransformer is the exception, because it is not isolating. Its two reference bushings are tied, so a neutral-to-phase transition on that shared reference is rejected.

Neutral-only grounding shunts are valid. The separate galvanic-island voltage-reference check may still flag an otherwise valid winding.

When roles cannot be assessed

The role rules apply to distinct, declared phase and neutral conductors. Ambiguous or unclassified roles, malformed declarations, multiple bus neutrals and earth conductors outside this model raise W.TMAP.CONNECTION_UNASSESSED with the reason. The declaration problem itself is reported by the terminal-convention checks. An element with invalid map dimensions is not shortened to make a position comparison possible.

Limits

  • The data-model arity warnings remain even where the native models accept a reduced-phase or implicit-reference winding. For example, an n_winding wye without an explicit neutral raises W.SPEC.XFMR_TMAP_ARITY.
  • The native n_winding solver accepts only the labels a, b, c, n, whatever roles are declared. Static role checks treat renamed maps equivalently; solver and primitive-admittance support is narrower.
  • Vector-group policy, mesh compatibility and a general earth or protective-earth wire model are outside these checks.

Endpoint evidence carries the subtype, side or winding index, bus, terminal map and positions, resolved roles and their source, the observed shape and the violated rule.
