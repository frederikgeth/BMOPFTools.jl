Capture sequence
----------------
1. tellegen-norwood-overview.jpg: open the case via the local file picker;
   Network > Show diagram; Geographic with Basemap on; initial whole-network
   view. No solve has been run in this image.
2. Run Solve AC power flow, then Analyse operating point in Studies.
   The viewer reports Converged, 13 iterations, Voltage band Valid, load voltage
   range 0.923–1.004 pu, source P/Q 26805.652 / 1057.165 kW/kvar, and passive loss
   699.585 kW. The overview reports 16 violations, 4 warnings, maximum loading
   344.2%, phase-neutral range 212.2–230.8 V, and phase-phase 10.78–11 kV.
   Overview branch-loss aggregation separately displays 699.55 kW; it is not
   substituted for the solver summary's passive-loss value.
3. Select the 184.3% transformer issue:
   sapn:norwood:inferred-transformer:e4c0af231a489a7bd7be
   tellegen-norwood-equipment.jpg: Geographic at the selection's reveal scale;
   Equipment and topology colouring; Separate overlapping buses and lines on.
4. tellegen-norwood-neighbourhood.jpg: the same selection and solve, three minus
   key zoom steps out from the equipment view, with the diagram focused.
5. tellegen-norwood-schematic.jpg: switch to Schematic (Tidy), reselect the same
   issue to reveal it, choose Loading, % of rating. Its network tag says
   135 buses, minimum 225.2 V and 184% loading; transformer label says 184.3%.
6. tellegen-norwood-results.jpg: same schematic and solve; scroll Studies to
   the conductor bars and multi-wire view. Currents are approximately 30.6 A
   per phase at the from end and 841.2 A per phase at the to end.

The screenshots are unretouched browser viewport captures. The input is not
edited, and no additional battery, voltage limit, or rating was inserted.
Norwood has no declared engineering voltage limits. Solver load-voltage-band
status is distinct from compliance with an engineering voltage bound.
Import notes include READ.BMOPF.SCHEMA_ABSENT and READ.BMOPF.RETAINED_SOURCE_ONLY
for component metadata retained in extras. Opening and solving do not establish
BMOPFTools schema conformance, electrical fidelity, or operating qualification.

The source model is provisional: its transformer roots, winding/conductor/phase/
MEN parameters and demand placement are authored study assumptions. It is not
an as-operated asset register or an individually qualified customer/equipment
reconstruction. Screenshots render the case; this repository does not copy its
82 MB model JSON. The browser's local-file workflow is documented at:
https://eigenergy.github.io/tellegen/local-case-files.html
